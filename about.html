About 2Fast2Clean

2Fast2Clean is a Pretoria, Centurion-based premium car wash focused on convenience, transparency, and quality.
Our online booking platform lets you reserve a time slot in seconds, choose a wash package priced clearly in ZAR, and arrive at a time that works for you —
no long queues, no guesswork, and no surprise costs. Whether you’re heading to a meeting in the CBD, dropping kids at school, or wrapping up a busy day, you can plan your car wash around your schedule instead of waiting in line.
Every vehicle is treated with professional care using quality products, and our team is trained to deliver a consistent, high-standard finish on every wash. With digital confirmations, easy rebooking, and a customer-first approach,
2Fast2Clean is designed to make keeping your car spotless as simple and stress-free as possible.

We’re inspired by modern digital service platforms but built specifically for South African drivers,
with local pricing, familiar payment options, and a booking experience that feels natural on mobile.
Instead of trying to copy overseas models directly, we’ve adapted the idea for Pretoria’s pace of life,
traffic patterns, and customer expectations — from clearly marked ZAR prices to flexible options like “pay in person” or secure online checkout.
Our goal is to give you the same level of polish and convenience you’d expect from a global brand, while still feeling grounded, accessible,
and relevant to everyday South Africans who just want a clean car without the hassle. .
