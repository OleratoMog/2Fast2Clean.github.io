Our Services

All prices are listed in South African Rand (ZAR). Simply choose the package that suits your budget and vehicle,
then proceed to checkout to confirm your booking. 2Fast2Clean currently operates primarily in Centurion, Pretoria,
serving drivers in the greater Tshwane area with fast, reliable and professional car care.

Express Wash — “In & out clean”
What it is: A fast, high-quality exterior clean for drivers who just want their car to look fresh without spending a lot of time. What we do: Pre-rinse to remove loose dust and road dirt Snow-foam pre-wash to gently lift grime High-pressure rinse for body and wheels Hand wash with pH-balanced shampoo Rinse and microfibre hand-dry to prevent water spots Tyre and wheel face clean with light tyre shine
Best for: Weekly/bi-weekly maintenance washes, company cars or anyone who wants a clean, shiny vehicle in about 25–30 minutes.

Standard Wash — “Inside & out refresh”
What it is: A balanced interior + exterior clean for everyday use. You drive away with a car that looks good from the outside and feels fresh inside. What we do (includes everything from Express Wash, plus):
Full interior vacuum (seats, carpets, boot area) Floor mats removed, cleaned and refitted Windows cleaned inside and out for better visibility Dash, centre console and door panels lightly wiped and dusted Door jambs and entrance sills wiped down
Best for: Families, ride-share drivers, or anyone who spends time in the car and wants it to feel clean and tidy. Average time: 35–45 minutes depending on vehicle size.

Premium Detail — “Showroom treatment”
What it is: A deep-clean and detailing service that restores your car’s look and feel, inside and out. This is more than a wash — it’s a full detail. What we do (includes everything from Standard Wash, plus): Interior:
Deep vacuum of seats, carpets, boot and hard-to-reach areas Carpet & fabric spot cleaning (light stains) Leather cleaned and conditioned (if applicable) Plastics, trims and vents carefully cleaned and dressed Interior deodorising for a fresher cabin
Exterior:
Bug and tar removal from front bumper and lower panels Hand clean of door jambs, fuel flap and other hidden areas Light paint decontamination to remove bonded grime Hand-applied wax or sealant for added gloss and protection Extra attention to wheels and tyres for a sharp finish
Best for: Selling your car, special occasions, or every few months to keep your vehicle in top condition. Plan for about 90–120 minutes, depending on the condition and size of the vehicle.
